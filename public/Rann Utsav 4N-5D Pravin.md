## **<mark>FOLLOWING YOUR DAY WISE SCHEDULED ITINERARY FOR THIS TOUR.</mark>** 

# **Day 1 | 04 Feb 2026 – Bhuj Railway Station to Dhordo** 

**City:** Dhordo **Hotel:** Kutch Resort **Room Type:** Premium Tent **Meal Plan:** MAP 

### **Route & Distance** 

- Bhuj Railway Station → Dhordo 

- **Approx. Distance:** 80 km 

- **Approx. Travel Time:** 2 to 2.5 hours 

### **Itinerary** 

- Arrival at Bhuj Railway Station 

- Meet & greet by driver 

- Drive to Dhordo – the Rann Utsav area 

- Check-in at Kutch Resort 

- Lunch at resort 

- Visit **White Rann of Kutch** 

- Enjoy evening **Rann Utsav cultural programs** (folk dance, music & activities) 

- Dinner and overnight stay at Dhordo 

# **Day 2 | 05 Feb 2026 – Dhordo to Dholavira** 

**City:** Dholavira **Hotel:** StayGuru Heaven Resort Dholavira **Room Type:** Non-AC Rajwadi Cottage **Meal Plan:** MAP 

### **Route & Distance** 

- Dhordo → Dholavira 

- **Approx. Distance:** 95 km 

- **Approx. Travel Time:** 2.5 to 3 hours 

### **Itinerary** 

- Breakfast at resort 

- Drive towards Dholavira 

- Check-in at hotel 

- Visit **Dholavira Harappan Archaeological Site (UNESCO World Heritage Site)** 

- Explore ancient reservoirs and citadel 

- Sunset view at the salt desert area 

- Dinner and overnight stay at Dholavira 

# **Day 3 | 06 Feb 2026 – Dholavira to Mandvi Beach** 

**City:** Mandvi Beach **Hotel:** Hotel Seven Beach Inn **Room Type:** Super Deluxe **Meal Plan:** CP 

### **Route & Distance** 

- Dholavira → Mandvi Beach 

- **Approx. Distance:** 190 km 

- **Approx. Travel Time:** 4.5 to 5 hours 

### **Itinerary** 

- Breakfast at hotel 

- Drive to Mandvi via Bhuj 

- Check-in at hotel 

- Leisure time at **Mandvi Beach** 

- Optional visit to **Vijay Vilas Palace (outside view)** 

- Sunset at Arabian Sea 

- Overnight stay at Mandvi Beach 

# **Day 4 | 07 Feb 2026 – Mandvi Beach to Bhuj** 

**City:** Bhuj **Hotel:** Hotel Mangalam **Room Type:** Standard AC Room **Meal Plan:** MAP 

### **Route & Distance** 

- Mandvi → Bhuj 

- **Approx. Distance:** 60 km 

- **Approx. Travel Time:** 1.5 to 2 hours 

### **Itinerary** 

- Breakfast at hotel 

- Drive to Bhuj 

- Visit **Aina Mahal** 

- Visit **Prag Mahal** 

- Explore **Bhuj Museum** 

- Shopping at Bhuj local handicraft market 

- Check-in at hotel 

- Dinner and overnight stay at Bhuj 

# **Day 5 | 08 Feb 2026 – Bhuj Railway Station Drop** 

### **Route & Distance** 

- Hotel → Bhuj Railway Station 

- **Approx. Distance:** 2 to 5 km 

- **Approx. Travel Time:** 10 to 15 minutes 

### **Itinerary** 

- Breakfast at hotel 

- Check-out 

- Drop at Bhuj Railway Station as per train schedule 

- Tour ends with beautiful memories of Kutch
