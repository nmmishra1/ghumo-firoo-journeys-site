Planning a Europe (Schengen) trip from India? Use these practical tips to improve your tourist visa approval chances.

## Documents & Financials
- Valid passport (min. 6 months beyond travel dates) with 2 blank pages
- Completed application form and appointment confirmation
- Bank statements (6 months), ITRs/Form 16, salary slips (if employed)
- Cover letter explaining itinerary, purpose, and funding
- Trip cost proof: flight reservations, hotel bookings, day-by-day plan

## Travel Insurance
- Schengen-compliant insurance with coverage of €30,000 minimum
- Cover all travel days (entry to exit)

## Itinerary Proof
- Realistic routing (avoid back-and-forth city hops)
- Pre-book major attractions to show genuine intent
- Include internal transport (trains/low-cost flights) if possible

## Sponsorships
- If sponsored, include sponsor’s bank statements, ID, and letter of support
- Relationship proof (for family sponsors)

## Interview Readiness
- Be concise and confident; answer exactly what’s asked
- Know your day-wise plan and funding source
- Show ties to India: job, business, family, property

## Smart Tips
- Apply via the country of longest stay (or first entry if equal)
- Avoid last-minute applications; aim 4–6 weeks before travel
- Keep copies of all documents and bookings
