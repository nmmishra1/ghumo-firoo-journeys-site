Experience the magic of **Rann Utsav 2025-26**, Gujarat's most spectacular cultural festival set in the mesmerizing White Desert of Kutch. From October 23, 2025, to March 4, 2026, witness 130+ days of cultural extravaganza at the iconic **Tent City Dhordo**.

## What is Rann Utsav? The Ultimate White Desert Experience

**Rann Utsav** is India's largest desert festival, conceived by Hon'ble Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi to showcase Gujarat's rich cultural heritage on the global tourism map. This annual cultural festival transforms the endless white salt desert into a vibrant celebration of life, art, and tradition.

### Why Rann Utsav is a Must-Visit Destination in 2025-26

The **White Desert of Kutch** offers an otherworldly experience that you won't find anywhere else in India. As Gujarat Tourism's ambassador Amitabh Bachchan famously says, "Kuch nahi dekha toh kuch nahi dekha" (if you haven't seen Kutch, you haven't seen anything).
