Preparing for the Char Dham Yatra? Use this practical packing checklist tailored for Himalayan terrain and temple protocols.

## Documents
- Government ID (Aadhaar/Passport)
- Permits, hotel vouchers, emergency contacts
- Travel insurance (recommended)

## Clothing
- Layered warm wear: fleece, light down jacket
- Quick-dry tees, trekking pants, thermals (early/late season)
- Rain jacket/poncho, cap, gloves, woolen socks
- Modest attire for temple visits

## Footwear
- Waterproof trekking shoes with grip
- Comfortable slippers for hotel/temple areas

## Health & Safety
- Personal medicines, altitude care, ORS
- First aid kit: band-aids, antiseptic, pain relief
- Sunscreen, lip balm, hand sanitizer, wet wipes

## Gear & Accessories
- Daypack (20–30L), reusable water bottle
- Torch/headlamp, power bank, extra cables
- Lightweight umbrella, quick-dry towel
- Small lock for luggage

## Food & Snacks
- Energy bars, dry fruits, glucose
- Reusable cutlery and steel mug (optional)

## Temple Etiquette
- Follow queue systems; respect local customs
- Avoid plastic litter; carry waste bag
- Remove shoes where required; maintain silence within sanctum

## Bonus Tips
- Keep cash for remote areas; ATMs may be limited
- Check weather and road status; keep buffer days
- Opt for porter/pony services if needed at Kedarnath
