**<mark>FOLLOWING YOUR DAY WISE SCHEDULED ITINERARY FOR THIS TOUR.</mark>** 

## **Day 1: - Pick-up from Bhuj Airport / Railway Station and bhuj Sight seen.** 

- ❖ Check-in at traditional stay in Kutch (Bhunga / Cottage / Tent). 

- ❖ Afternoon (3:00 PM): Visit Rann Utsav – explore craft stalls & enjoy photography. 

- ❖ Experience White Rann Desert activities ( **camel ride, cart ride – direct payment** ). 

- ❖ Witness the Sunset at White Rann. 

- ❖ Evening: Bonfire with Kutchi folk musical program (subject to resort arrangements). 

- ❖ Dinner & Overnight stay in Kutch. 

## **Day 2 – Dholavira / Road to Heaven / Kalo Dungar** 

- ❖ Early morning visit to White Rann Sunrise (6:30 AM). 

- ❖ Breakfast at resort. 

- ❖ Drive to Road to Heaven (scenic stretch with sea & rann views). 

- ❖ Visit Dholavira – Harappan Civilization archaeological site. 

- ❖ Visit Kalo Dungar (Black Hill) – highest point in Kutch, panoramic views, Guru Dattatreya Temple, and Magnetic Hill experience. 

- ❖ Return to Bhuj Airport/Railway Station. 

## **Day 3 – Dholavira / Road to Heaven / Kalo Dungar** 

- ❖ After breakfast, check out and proceed towards **Bhuj** . On arrival, visit the **Smrutivan Earthquake Memorial Museum** . Later, continue to **Mandvi** where you will relax at **Mandvi Beach** and visit the **Vijay Vilas Palace** , a beautiful example of royal Kutchi architecture
